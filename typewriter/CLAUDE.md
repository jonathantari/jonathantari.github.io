# Typewriter — notes for Claude Code

Single-file app: `index.html` (CSS, markup and JS inline) plus `sounds.mp3`. Served at garage.jonathantari.com/typewriter from this hub repo (GitHub Pages, main branch).

- Read `BACKLOG.md` first: it holds what's built, the design rules and what's next.
- The feel is calm and quiet: low contrast, slow motion, nothing that pops. Prefer text and line drawings on the paper over panels, cards or white backgrounds. The options panel and save menu are the only cards; don't add new ones.
- Timer: see the "Writing timer" section in `BACKLOG.md` — no popover panel; hover grows the dial and shows the time in grey inside it, with "+5 min" / "end" as plain text links beside it.
- Feedback and the centred title: see their sections in `BACKLOG.md`.
- Keep it simple: no stats, streaks, history or settings beyond what's listed.
