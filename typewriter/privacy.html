Your words are yours.

Everything you type stays on your device: in this browser, or in files you choose to save. It is never sent to us. We can't read it, and we never will.

If you connect Google Drive, your files travel straight between your browser and your own Drive. Nobody else is in between.

The little we do count

To know whether anyone is enjoying this typewriter, we count a few anonymous things:

  • that someone visited
  • how many words were typed. Just the number, never the words.
  • general details like device type, browser and country

This is done with Umami, a privacy-friendly analytics service hosted in the EU. No cookies, nothing stored on your device for tracking, and nothing that follows you to other sites. Your IP address is only used to work out the country, then forgotten. Nothing is sold, shared or used for ads.

Rather not be counted? Send a note through the feedback button and I'll take you out of the stats. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, you're never counted at all.

Questions? Use the feedback button in options.
