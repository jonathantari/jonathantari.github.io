Entrepreneurship through acquisition (ETA) is the taking over of an existing business and leading it into its next chapter.

Join the next cohort How it works

Keeping a good business alive is a public good.

A generation of owners is retiring. Every year, hundreds of thousands of good businesses with real customers are closing because nobody is ready to take over.

The entrepreneurs who take them into the next generation are doing their community a service,
which is why their community should help them do it.

In the EU alone, around 150,000 businesses and 600,000 jobs are at risk each year (European Commission). In the US, 2.3 million businesses owned by retiring baby boomers employ 24.7 million people (Federal Reserve Bank of Chicago).
